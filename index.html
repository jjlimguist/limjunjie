JJ Lim

Hi! I'm currently a Postdoctoral Fellow in Linguistics at the National University of Singapore. I received my Ph.D. from UC San Diego and I previously taught at California State University San Marcos.

Email | CV | Scholar

Research

I am a syntactician interested in morphosyntactic theory, variation, and change. I have worked on topics including nominalisation, case assignment, reduplication, pronominal structure, and contact-induced change.

In my work, I draw on original data collected through a range of methodologies, including fieldwork, collaborative resource creation, corpus development, and (psycho)linguistic experiments.

My research has investigated languages including Kalmyk Oirat, Khalkha Mongolian, Korean, Megrelian, Singapore English (Singlish), and Turkish.
