Research

I am a syntactician interested in morphosyntactic theory, variation, and change. I have worked on topics including nominalisation, case assignment, number marking, pronominal structure, and contact-induced change.

In my work, I draw on original data collected through a range of methodologies, including fieldwork, collaborative resource creation, corpus development, and (psycho)linguistic experiments.

I have worked on languages including Khalkha Mongolian, Kalmyk Oirat, Korean, Turkish, Singapore English (Singlish), and Megrelian.
